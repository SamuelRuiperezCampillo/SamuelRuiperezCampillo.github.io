About — 🚧 Website in Progress

Samuel Ruipérez-Campillo is an engineer and PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Institute of Machine Learning, ETH Zurich, where his research focuses on machine learning for cardiology. He is currently a visiting research fellow at MIT’s Institute for Medical Engineering and Science and Harvard Medical School. Previously, he completed an MSc at ETH Zurich and an MEng at UC Berkeley, worked at Stanford under Prof. Sanjiv Narayan, joined PhysCade Inc., a Stanford spin-off developing AI for electrophysiology, as its third employee, and founded SWiiFT Inc., a Berkeley SkyDeck startup. His studies have been fully funded by several competitive fellowships, including the “la Caixa” Fellowship, the Rafael del Pino Fellowship, and the UC Berkeley Fung Institute Fellowship.

Research

My research applies machine learning and signal processing to cardiology, developing generative and representation-learning models for cardiac signals such as the ECG and intracardiac electrograms. A central focus is cardiac electrophysiology — mapping arrhythmogenic substrates and characterizing cardiac activation from multi-electrode recordings. As a PhD candidate at the Institute of Machine Learning at ETH Zurich, currently a visiting research fellow at MIT and Harvard Medical School, I work on translating these methods into clinical practice.
